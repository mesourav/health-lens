# Doctors, Networks and Referrals

## Do I need a referral to see a specialist?
No. With a PPO plan you can book a specialist, such as a dermatologist or cardiologist,
directly without a referral from your primary care doctor.

## What is in-network and out-of-network?
In-network doctors and hospitals have agreed prices with us, so you pay less. Out-of-network
care is still covered on PPO plans, but your share of the cost is higher and you may be billed
the difference between the provider's price and what the plan allows.

## How do I find an in-network doctor?
Use the Find a Doctor tool in the member portal or app, or ask a member advocate. Always confirm
with the office that they are in-network for your specific plan before your visit.

## What about emergencies?
Emergency room care is covered at the in-network rate even at an out-of-network hospital.
You never need prior approval for an emergency. Call your local emergency number if you have a
life-threatening emergency.

## Can I use telehealth?
Yes. Video visits with in-network doctors and therapists cost the same as in-person visits.
