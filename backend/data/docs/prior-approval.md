# Prior Approval Guide

## What is prior approval?
Prior approval (also called prior authorization) means we confirm with your doctor that a test,
procedure, or medicine is medically needed before you receive it. It protects you from surprise
bills for care the plan would not pay for.

## Which services need prior approval?
MRI, CT and PET scans, planned hospital stays, outpatient surgery, sleep studies, and some
specialty medicines need prior approval. Emergency care never needs prior approval.

## How long does prior approval take?
Most requests are decided in 2 to 3 business days. Urgent requests are decided within 72 hours.
We send a text message and an email as soon as a decision is made.

## Who asks for prior approval?
Your doctor's office usually sends the request for you. You can ask your doctor's office whether
it has been submitted, or contact a member advocate to check the status.

## What happens if I skip prior approval?
If you receive a service that needed prior approval without getting it first, the claim may be
denied and you could be billed for the full cost. If that happens, you can file an appeal.
