# Claims and Billing

## How do claims work?
When you get care from an in-network provider, the provider sends the claim to us directly.
We review it, pay our share, and the provider bills you for the rest. You usually do not need
to do anything.

## How long does a claim take?
Most claims are processed within 14 days of when we receive them. A claim that is "in review"
may need more information from your provider.

## What is an Explanation of Benefits (EOB)?
An EOB is a summary we send after processing a claim. It shows what the provider billed, what
the plan paid, and what you owe. It is not a bill.

## How do I appeal a denied claim?
You can appeal within 180 days of the denial. Submit the appeal form in the member portal with
any supporting letters from your doctor. Most appeals are decided within 30 days.

## How do I pay my premium?
You can pay your monthly premium in the member portal, set up autopay, or pay by phone.
Premiums are due on the first day of each month, with a 30-day grace period.
