# Understanding Your Costs

## What is a deductible?
The deductible is the amount you pay each plan year for covered care before the plan starts
sharing the cost. The deductible resets every January 1.

## What counts toward the deductible?
Most in-network care you pay for counts: specialist visits, lab tests, imaging, and hospital
stays. Preventive care such as your yearly physical is free and does not count toward the
deductible. Prescriptions have their own copays and do not count toward the deductible.

## What is coinsurance?
Coinsurance is the percentage of the cost you pay after your deductible is met. For example,
with 20% coinsurance on a $1,000 bill, you pay $200 and the plan pays $800.

## What is a copay?
A copay is a fixed amount you pay for a visit, such as $30 for a therapy session. Copays apply
whether or not you have met your deductible.

## What is the out-of-pocket maximum?
The out-of-pocket maximum is the most you will pay for covered in-network care in a plan year.
Your deductible, copays and coinsurance all count toward it. After you reach it, the plan pays
100% of covered in-network care for the rest of the year.
